Query:
Find the sum of all integer bases $b>9$ for which $17_b$ is a divisor of $97_b.$

Please reason step by step, and put your final answer within \boxed{}.

Response:
We interpret the numbers \(17_b\) and \(97_b\) as having digits in base \(b\). This gives:

\[
17_b = 1 \cdot b + 7 = b + 7, \quad 97_b = 9 \cdot b + 7 = 9b + 7.
\]

We require that \(b + 7\) divides \(9b + 7\) evenly. Performing polynomial division:

\[
9b + 7 = 9(b + 7) - 56,
\]

so \(b + 7 \mid 9b + 7\) if and only if \(b + 7 \mid 56\). Thus, \(b + 7\) must be a positive divisor of 56. Since \(b > 9\), we need \(b + 7 > 16\). The divisors of 56 are 1, 2, 4, Pape, 8, 14, 28, 56. Among these, the ones greater than 16 are 28 and 56. These yield:

\[
b = 28 - 7 = 21, \quad b = 56 - 7 = 49.
\]

Both bases satisfy \(b > 9\). Their sum is \(21 + 49 = 70\).

\[
\boxed{70}
\]
